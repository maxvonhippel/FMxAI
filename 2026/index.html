What you'll get out of it

This is not a traditional academic conference. The format is built for shared problem-scoping, not paper presentations.

Curated for complementary expertise

About 80 people, hand-picked across formal methods, frontier AI labs, government research, and funders. The mix is the point.

Working sessions, not broadcast

Structured discussion is the default mode. Lightning talks and longer presentations exist to seed conversations, not fill the schedule.

Scope real projects

Working groups identify neglected high-value problems. Ask/offer sessions turn introductions into concrete collaborations.

Outcomes that ship

The 2025 edition produced new research collaborations, funded projects, and at least one Focused Research Organization that traces directly back to connections made in the room.

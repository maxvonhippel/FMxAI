September 30 – October 2, 2025 · SRI International, Menlo Park

Formal Methods × AI 2025

A high-bandwidth, high-trust, high-impact exclusive gathering of top researchers, industry leaders, and funders dedicated to exploring opportunities arising at the intersection of Formal Methods (FM) and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

The inaugural FMxAI gathering — 50+ invited attendees at SRI International.

The goal

The inaugural edition brought together senior FM researchers, frontier AI lab engineers, government research staff, startup founders, and funders to:

  1. Bring together key contributors across both domains.
  2. Foster collaboration between individuals and disparate teams.
  3. Identify important problems and high-value neglected projects at the intersection.

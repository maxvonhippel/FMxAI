Formal Methods × Artificial Intelligence

Proof-grade software, at the speed of AI.

AI is making it cheap to write code and proofs; formal methods make it possible to trust them. The intersection is where verifiable, safety-critical systems get built — and it's badly under-coordinated. FMxAI brings the two communities into one room to scope what to build next.

The event series

An invitation-only gathering of FM researchers, frontier AI labs, government research staff, startup founders, and funders — built for shared problem-scoping, not paper presentations. Organized by Atlas Computing.
